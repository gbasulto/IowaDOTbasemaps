# IowaDOTbasemaps 0.1.0

- Three live Iowa DOT Web Maps with feature and label layers.
- CARTO Positron, Dark Matter and Voyager with vector/raster modes and API key.
- Leaflet widget/proxy integration, managed replacement and removal.
- Low, noninteractive basemap panes and cancellation of stale responses.
- Explicit fallback, metadata timeouts and map/Shiny status reporting.
- Catalog checks, refresh and replacement-ID workflow.
- Shiny regression app, R tests, and maintenance instructions.
- Initial validation limitations are recorded in VALIDATION.md.
